What each Kinect example does, and how. Pick one, then watch its steps run on a simulated sensor.

    Try it on the real Kinect

    The room, the person and the depth noise are simulated at a quarter of the Kinect's resolution, using the examples' own settings and logic. Numbers drawn on the stage come from that simulation; the measured ones are in exercises/cpp/README.md.
